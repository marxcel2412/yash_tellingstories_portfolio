## Is the Debt Divide Widening?
### Government Debt Distribution Across OECD Countries | 1995–2024

How has the distribution of government debt changed across OECD countries over the past three decades? This visualization looks beyond individual country rankings to investigate whether debt burdens are becoming more uneven, particularly around the 2008 financial crisis and the COVID-19 pandemic.
