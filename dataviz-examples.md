# Data visualization examples

### General Government Debt as a Percentage of GDP (2010–2025)

These visualizations compare general government debt across OECD countries in 2010, 2015, 2020, and 2025, expressed as a percentage of GDP. The charts illustrate differences in national debt burdens and how they have evolved over time, including the sharp increases experienced by many countries during the COVID-19 pandemic. They also highlight variations in fiscal recovery and debt levels across countries.
